Website privacy choices

This notice explains the public website statistics provided by Tame Dynamics for newphase-coaching.com.

Basic page totals

We keep a daily total of public page loads, including when you decline optional analytics. This separate counter stores only the website, date and total. It does not store visitor identifiers, IP addresses, page addresses or individual browsing histories. Repeated loads and bots may increase the total; blockers and connection failures may reduce it.

Optional website analytics

Optional analytics starts automatically for visits recognised as Australian. Elsewhere, or when location is uncertain, it stays off unless you allow it. Choose No optional analytics to stop future optional collection. Your previous choice and browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are respected. Your choice is saved on this browser for up to 180 days and can be changed using Privacy choices.

Optional analytics measures public page views, approximate location, device category, referring sites and enquiry actions. It receives no form contents, names, email addresses, query strings or payment details. Umami processes network information to derive temporary session and location information; the analytics database does not retain raw IP addresses. These are statistical measurements, not proof of unique people or completed sales.

The analytics collector is operated by Tame Dynamics in Sydney. Hosting, delivery and security providers may separately process network addresses and security logs. A country-only check uses the collector's Cloudflare edge; its trace details are not retained by this website. Private application, account and sensitive form routes are excluded. We do not use this analytics setup for advertising or cross-site personal profiles.

Earlier optional measurements are not automatically erased when you change your choice. For privacy enquiries, use the contact details on this website.
